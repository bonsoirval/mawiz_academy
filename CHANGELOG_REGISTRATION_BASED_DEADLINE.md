# Registration-based program deadline countdown

- Course/program deadlines now derive from the learner account registration timestamp, rather than the date they enroll in or open an individual course.
- The course catalog displays each course deadline and days remaining for signed-in learners.
- The course detail/program page displays the deadline and countdown, including due-today and overdue states.
- Existing enrollment deadlines are reconciled to the registration-based schedule when the learner views the course catalog or detail page.
- Course completion progress and enrollment start timestamps remain separate from the registration-based deadline clock.
