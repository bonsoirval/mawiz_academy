ABOUT MAWIZ

Building the bridge between learning and meaningful work.

Mawiz Academy is an AI-assisted learning platform where emerging professionals learn a skill, practice it, complete realistic projects and workplace simulations, receive feedback, and build evidence of readiness for professional work.

MAWIZ ACADEMY

Learning that leads somewhere.

Mawiz Academy combines learning, practice, workplace simulation and capability evidence. The goal is not thousands of shallow courses; it is a system that produces demonstrably capable people.

MAWIZ TECHNOLOGIES

The company behind the platform.

Mawiz Technologies Ltd is the company identified in the Academy functional specification. Mawiz Academy is its focused product for developing practical and workplace-ready capability.

MISSION

Help people cross the gap.

Help learners move from “I learned it” to “I can actually work” by combining guided learning with practice, projects, simulations, feedback and evidence.

VISION

A clearer path from capability to opportunity.

The long-term product loop connects learning, capability, portfolio evidence and eventually real work and opportunity.
