Platform

Deadline penalty & access

When a learner's registration-based course deadline expires, learning content is locked until the penalty payment is verified. Set a penalty amount and the number of days access resumes for. Use 0 days to grant the course's original duration from the payment date.

Referral program & payouts

Set the course commission paid to referrers and the minimum balance required before a payout request can be submitted.

Homepage content limits

Control how many published programs and courses appear on the public homepage. Set a limit to 0 to hide that section's listings, or up to 50 items.

Contact page & social links

These details appear on the public Contact page. Paste your Google Maps share link in the map field (the page will use it as the visitor link and generate an embed from your address), or paste an embed URL. Add the directions URL separately.
